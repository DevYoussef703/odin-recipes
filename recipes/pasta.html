Garlic Butter Pasta

A simple, comforting pasta dish coated in rich melted butter and fragrant garlic. It takes less than 20 minutes to make and has a savory flavor that's satisfying on its own or as a side dish. Think of it as the food equivalent of a cozy blanket.

ingredients

  • 200 g pasta
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper
  • Parmesan cheese (optional)
  1. Cook the pasta according to package instructions.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan and sauté the garlic for about 1 minute.
  3. Add the drained pasta and toss well.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Top with Parmesan and serve.
