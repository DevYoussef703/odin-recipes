Banana Pancakes

Soft, naturally sweet pancakes made with just a few ingredients. The banana gives them a fluffy texture and a hint of sweetness, making them perfect for breakfast or a quick snack. They're healthier than traditional pancakes and surprisingly filling.

ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ tsp cinnamon (optional)
  1. Mash the banana in a bowl.
  2. Mix in the eggs until smooth.
  3. Pour small circles onto a lightly oiled pan over medium heat.
  4. Cook 1-2 minutes per side.
  5. Serve with honey or fruit.
