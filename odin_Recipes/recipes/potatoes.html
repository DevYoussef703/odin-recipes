Crispy Oven Potatoes

Golden-brown potato wedges with a crunchy exterior and a fluffy interior. Seasoned with paprika and roasted until crisp, they're a delicious alternative to fries and pair well with burgers, chicken, or dipping sauces.

ingredients

  • 4 potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper
  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  2. Cut potatoes into wedges.
  3. Toss with oil, paprika, salt, and pepper
  4. Spread on a baking tray.
  5. Bake for 35-40 minutes until golden and crispy.
